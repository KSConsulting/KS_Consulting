Uppercase pill for status and category markers; keep the text to one or two words.

```jsx
<Badge tone="neon" dot>Ao vivo</Badge>
<Badge tone="blue" variant="solid">Novo</Badge>
```

Tones: blue (default/product), neon (result, opportunity), cyan (data), neutral, danger. `soft` is tinted-on-dark; `solid` fills with the tone colour and is loud — one per view.
