One-line hover explainer; wraps its trigger.

```jsx
<Tooltip content="Custo por aquisição nos últimos 30 dias">
  <IconButton label="Ajuda" icon={<Icon name="info" size={16} />} />
</Tooltip>
```

12px text on the elevated surface, 8px radius, fades in over 250ms. Keep it to a single line — anything longer belongs in the interface.
