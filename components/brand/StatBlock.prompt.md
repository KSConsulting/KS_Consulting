Giant figure + tiny caption. This is how the brand proves performance — use three to four across a hero or results band, never a paragraph of numbers.

```jsx
<StatBlock value={500} suffix="+" label="Projetos entregues" animate />
<StatBlock value="24/7" tone="neon" size="md" label="Monitoramento" />
```

`tone="neon"` marks the single result you most want remembered; the others stay white. Count-up runs 1200ms with an ease-out cubic.
