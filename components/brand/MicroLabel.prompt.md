The brand's section eyebrow: a numbered, uppercase, wide-tracked micro-label sitting above every headline.

```jsx
<MicroLabel index="01">Performance</MicroLabel>
<MicroLabel index="03" tone="neon" separator="/">Resultado</MicroLabel>
```

12px, 600 weight, .14em tracking, muted grey text with the index in blue (or neon when the section is about results). Never larger than 12px — the scale contrast against the headline is the point.
